Sprint Planning

Structured 2–4 hour sessions with clear sprint goal definition, capacity-based commitment, and DoR validation before any story enters the sprint.

01Review sprint goal with PO & stakeholders
02Validate team capacity (holidays, leave)
03Confirm DoR for each candidate story
04Commit sprint backlog · document risks
☀️

Daily Standup

15-minute focused sync. Async-first model for distributed teams. Impediments logged in Jira within the same day for immediate escalation.

01Progress since yesterday
02Today's plan & commitments
03Blockers & dependencies flagged
04SM escalates blockers within 4 hours
🔄

Retrospective

Blameless retros using rotating formats — 4Ls, Sailboat, STOP/START/CONTINUE. Max 3 action items per sprint with named DRIs and tracked closure.

01Review previous retro actions (closure)
02Open retrospective activity (30 min)
03Vote & prioritise improvement items
04Assign ≤3 actions with DRI & due date
📋

RAID Management

Live RAID log maintained in Confluence. Weekly review with stakeholders. Risks quantified by probability × impact matrix with mitigation ownership.

01Log Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Deps
02Score risks (P × I matrix)
03Assign mitigation owner & deadline
04Review in weekly stakeholder sync
🎯

Backlog Grooming

Weekly refinement ensuring top 2 sprints of backlog meet DoR. Stories rated against INVEST criteria. Technical risks surfaced before sprint commitment.

01PO presents prioritised candidates
02Team estimates via Planning Poker
03AC reviewed and agreed in session
04DoR checklist completed per story
🗺️

Dependency Management

Cross-team dependency maps maintained in Jira. Weekly dependency stand-up with tech leads. Critical path visualised in Confluence for programme-wide transparency.

01Map inter-squad story dependencies
02Assign dependency owner per squad
03Review in PI Planning / programme sync
04Escalate blocking deps within 24h

Philosophy

Leadership Principles

The mindset and principles that guide how I lead teams, manage delivery, and create lasting organisational impact.

🎯

Outcomes Over Outputs

Velocity is a vanity metric. What matters is business value delivered per sprint — not story points closed.

I anchor every delivery conversation to measurable outcomes. Sprint planning starts with "what business problem are we solving?" not "how many tickets can we fit?"

🛡️

Psychological Safety First

High-performing teams only exist where people feel safe to raise problems, challenge assumptions, and admit mistakes.

I build psychological safety through blameless retrospectives, protected team time, and visible escalation of systemic issues to leadership rather than individuals.

🌐

Distributed Team Excellence

Time zones are a feature, not a bug — async-first culture with intentional synchronous moments creates global delivery leverage.

Having coordinated teams across India, Singapore, Europe, and the US, I've developed a structured async-first model that maintains team cohesion across time zones.

📊

Data-Driven Transparency

Stakeholders deserve honesty, not optimism. A clear red RAG status with a mitigation plan builds more trust than a false green.

I maintain real-time Jira dashboards, weekly RAG reports, and proactive risk escalation — ensuring stakeholders always have accurate delivery intelligence.
